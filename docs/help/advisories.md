## What this page is for

The Advisory Email Generator turns a CVE and its affected assets into a professional, reviewable advisory
email. It includes an asset validation matrix, an Excel attachment and a unique `EXCAT-YYYY-NNNN` ID.

## How to use it

This module ships in **Phase 2**. Until then, **Create Advisory Email** on a CVE opens this page with the CVE
pre-selected, so you can confirm the hand-off works.

## Required permissions

`advisory:create` to draft, `advisory:approve` to approve, `advisory:send` to send and `advisory:export` to
download.
