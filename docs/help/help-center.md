## What this page is for

The Help Center brings together every in-app help topic. It only shows topics you have permission to use.

## How to use it

1. Type at least two characters in **Search help** to search across all topics.
2. Open **Glossary** for definitions of CVE, CVSS, EPSS, KEV, CWE, CPE, CTEM, SLA and more.
3. Open **What's new** for the changelog of the version you're running.
4. On any page, press the **?** button in the page header, or press `?` on your keyboard, to open that
   page's help.
5. Click **Full documentation** to open the complete docs site at `/docs`.
6. Click **Tour** in the header to replay the guided walkthrough for your role (Analyst, Approver, Admin or
   Viewer). It runs automatically on your first sign-in. Skip it with **Esc**, and move between steps with
   the arrow keys.

## Common errors

- **A topic is missing**: help topics follow your role. Ask an admin if you think you need more access.
